Pakistani Cuisine: Chicken Karahi

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 4-5 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 2-3 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Method

Heat oil in a wok, add cumin seeds, then onion and fry until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken pieces, cook until chicken changes color. Add chopped tomatoes, chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Cook on medium heat until chicken is done and oil separates. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.

Indian Cuisine: Butter Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, cut into cubes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Method

Melt butter in a pan, sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken, cook for 5–7 minutes. Add tomato puree, chili powder, salt, and garam masala. Simmer until chicken is fully cooked. Stir in cream at the end, cook for 2 more minutes, and serve hot.

Chinese Cuisine: Kung Pao Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast, diced
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
  • 3-4 dried red chilies
  • 2 green bell peppers, diced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

Mix chicken with cornstarch and a little soy sauce. Heat oil in a pan, stir-fry chilies, then add chicken until golden. Add bell peppers, peanuts, hoisin sauce, soy sauce, and vinegar. Stir until sauce thickens and chicken is fully cooked. Serve hot with rice.

Italian Cuisine: Margherita Pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

Preheat oven to 220°C. Roll out pizza dough, spread tomato sauce evenly. Top with sliced mozzarella and basil leaves. Drizzle olive oil and sprinkle salt. Bake for 10-12 minutes until crust is golden and cheese melts. Serve hot.
