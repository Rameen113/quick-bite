Recipe 2:Cheese and Mushroom Omelette

  1. 2 Eggs
  2. Butter
  3. Mushrooms of your choice
  4. Cheese of your choice

Method

Heat a small frying pan over medium heat. Add the butter and allow to melt, coating the pan.

Add eggs and mushrooms, gently folding edges toward the center until mostly set. Sprinkle cheese on top and cook 1–2 more minutes. Fold omelette and serve immediately.

Recipe 2: Avocado and Salami Toast

Ingredients

  1. 2 slices of bread
  2. 1 fresh avocado
  3. Salt and pepper
  4. Salami
  5. Optional: cherry tomatoes, olive oil, lemon juice

Method

Toast the bread slices. Mash the avocado with a fork, season with salt, pepper, and optional lemon juice. Spread over toast, add upto 2 salamis or as required, top with cherry tomatoes if desired.

Recipe 3: Greek Yogurt Fruit Parfait

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup Greek yogurt
  2. 1/2 cup granola
  3. 1/2 cup mixed berries or any fruits of your choice (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries)
  4. Honey/Maple Syrup (optional)

Method

In a glass or bowl, layer half of the yogurt, then half of the granola and berries/fruit. Repeat the layers. Drizzle honey on top if desired. Serve immediately.

Recipe 4: Veggie Quesidillas

Ingredients

  1. Frozen Vegetables
  2. Oil
  3. Tortilla Wraps
  4. Sauces: Ketchup, Mayonnaise and chilli sauce(siracha)
  5. Soy sauce or seasoning of choice

Method

Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add all vegetables and stir-fry for upto 5 minutes, then you can add your sauces and mix until cooked.Take a tortilla and add the mixture from your pan to the wrap and fold as per your liking. Serve hot.
